Internal Medicine PGY-3 • Aspiring Cardiologist

Bijal Rajput, MD

Internal Medicine Resident Physician • NYU Grossman School of Medicine / Bellevue Hospital

I am a PGY-3 Internal Medicine resident physician at NYU Langone Health and Bellevue Hospital, and a graduate of Harvard Medical School. As an aspiring cardiologist, my foremost commitment is delivering compassionate, rigorous, and patient-centered care at the bedside.

Alongside clinical training, my research centers on leveraging medical artificial intelligence and large language models to identify and mitigate diagnostic delays, as well as investigating critical care hemodynamics and transplant health policy. Ultimately, I aim to connect bedside clinical insights with meaningful innovation to improve everyday patient outcomes and care delivery.

Academic Interests & Collaborations

Research & Innovation

Clinical AI & Large Language Models

Developing and evaluating EHR-integrated LLM systems to identify diagnostic and management delays in acute coronary syndrome and venous thromboembolism.

• NYU Diagnosis Aid Team & Google Research collaboration

• EHR-integrated diagnostic feedback dashboards

Published in JHM & Am J Cardiol

Critical Care Cardiology & MCS

Investigating mechanical circulatory support hemodynamics, VA-ECMO stabilization in refractory arrhythmias, and post-transplant pericarditis therapies.

• VA-ECMO in obstructive HCM & VT Storm

• IL-1 inhibitors in post-transplant pericarditis

Best Poster Award, NYU CC Cardiology

Transplant Policy & Health Equity

Evaluating national thoracic allocation policies, disparities in simultaneous heart-kidney transplants, and donation-after-circulatory-death (DCD) donor modeling.

• National UNOS / OPTN safety net policy evaluations

• Novel DCD heart donor progression models
